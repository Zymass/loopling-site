How rewind works

Every swipe and wait you make during a loop is recorded. When you tap Rewind (or run out of moves), time scrubs back and you respawn at the start — and an amber echo replays your recorded moves tick for tick alongside you. Echoes press plates, hold doors, and collect shards, but they also occupy their cell: an echo can block your path, so plan moves that help future-you. When an echo's recording ends, it freezes on its final cell for the rest of the level. Rewinds within a level are limited (1–3 loops, shown as dots in the HUD), but restarting a level is free and unlimited.

FAQ

  • When does the daily puzzle change? The Daily Echo refreshes at midnight UTC, so the exact local time depends on your time zone. Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle each day, and today's daily is always free.
  • How do I restore my Pro purchase? Open Settings → Restore Purchases (also available on the paywall). Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Loopling Pro; the unlock is a one-time purchase tied to that account, so it carries over to new devices.
  • Why is my echo not moving the way I did? Echoes replay your inputs, not your path — if something now blocks a recorded step (a closed door, another body), the echo skips that step but stays on its timeline.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is stored on your device and is included in your normal device backup. Nothing is stored on our servers — there are none.
