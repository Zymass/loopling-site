Loopling

Time loop puzzles. Rewind, and an echo of you replays every move you made — solve each level together with your past self.

Privacy Policy Support

What it is

Each level is a small grid with plates, doors, shards, and an exit — but you only get a few moves per loop. Make your run count, then rewind: time scrubs back, you respawn, and a translucent amber echo replays your recorded moves tick for tick alongside you. Echoes press plates, hold doors open, collect shards — and physically block your path. The puzzle is planning moves that help future-you instead of trapping him.

60 handcrafted levels

Five chapters, from gentle first steps to par-tight master loops with three of you on the board at once. Chapters 1–2 are free. A single $3.99 purchase unlocks everything else — one payment, yours forever, no subscriptions.

The Daily Echo

One shared puzzle every day — the same grid for everyone in the world, refreshing at UTC midnight. Keep your streak alive and share your solve as a spoiler-free emoji grid. The daily puzzle is always free.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account. Your progress stays on your device and the game works fully offline. Purchases are processed by Apple; the only third-party code is the RevenueCat SDK, used solely to validate the one-time Pro purchase.
