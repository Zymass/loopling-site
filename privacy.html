Privacy Policy

Loopling · Last updated 12 June 2026

The short version

Loopling shows no ads, runs no analytics, does no tracking, and has no account or sign-in. Your puzzle progress stays on your device, and the game works fully offline. The only network activity is validating the optional one-time Pro purchase.

What stays on your device

Level progress, stars, daily streaks, and preferences are stored only on your device (and, if you use it, in your own iCloud-backed device backup via the standard system mechanisms). This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Loopling offers one optional purchase: Loopling Pro, a one-time non-consumable unlock. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. The app uses the RevenueCat SDK solely to validate that purchase: RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and the purchase receipt — nothing that identifies you personally. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. No purchase is required to play chapters 1–2 or the daily puzzle.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, sell, share, or process any personal data. There is no analytics or advertising SDK in the app.

Permissions

Loopling requests no permissions. It does not use location, the microphone, the camera, contacts, motion data, or notifications.

Children

Loopling is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone.
